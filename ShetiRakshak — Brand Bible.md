# ShetiRakshak — Brand Bible

*Maharashtra-first · Marathi-first · Cotton-first · Built to scale*

> **The heart:** "मला काय होतंय ते समजतं. मला आता काय करायचं ते समजतं. गरज पडली तर कोणाकडे जायचं तेही माहीत आहे."

Note: all agronomy, scheme and price content below is illustrative. Every live fact must come from verified sources and expert review (see Governance).

## 1. Strategic Territory

| Territory | Strength | Weakness |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A. The Protector | Fits the name | Reads as a shield cliché; promises outcomes we can't guarantee |
| B. The Farm Compass | Clear, action-oriented | Cold, tool-like; no human element |
| C. The Bridge | Captures experts, schemes, markets | Infrastructure language; no emotion |
| **D. The Watch (पहारा)** | **Chosen** | Needs discipline so it never over-promises |

**Chosen: THE WATCH — "पहारा".** Farmers have always kept *rakhan* over fields: someone awake so the farmer isn't alone with a risk. ShetiRakshak keeps watch over the **moment of decision**: weather turning, a leaf spotting, a scheme deadline, a market window. A watch doesn't claim to prevent the storm. It makes sure you see it coming, know your next step, and know who to call.

- **Why strongest:** it gives "Rakshak" a precise, honest meaning (protection = early awareness + a next step + human backup, never guarantees). It is emotional, local in feeling, and hard to copy.
- **Visually:** a watch line and a signal arch (section 6).
- **In UX:** every screen ends in a next step; uncertainty is shown; a human is always one tap away.
- **Scaling:** every crop, language and state has risks and decisions to watch over.

**The big question — why trust us?** Because we don't leave you with information. We (1) tell you what it means for *your* crop, (2) say exactly how sure we are and where it came from, (3) give one next step, and (4) hand you to a real person when we reach our limit.

## 2. Brand Strategy

- **Purpose:** No farmer should face a critical decision alone, confused or too late.
- **Vision:** A trusted watch over every farm in India, in every farmer's own language.
- **Mission:** Turn weather, crop problems, prices and schemes into clear Marathi guidance with a next step and real human help.
- **Promise:** *Understand. Know your next step. Know where to get help.*
- **Positioning:** The only farming companion that ends every answer with a next step and a person to ask. Not a weather app, portal or chatbot.
- **UVP:** Weather and crop problems explained as decisions, in Marathi, by voice, with honest confidence and verified human escalation.
- **Essence:** **Watchful. Clear. Next-step.**
- **Belief:** Farmers don't lack information, they lack understandable information at the right time. Technology should serve judgment, never replace it.
- **Enemy:** **Information without direction**: scattered, jargon-heavy, late, language-blocked advice.
- **Emotional shift:** Uncertain / overloaded / alone → Understands / knows the next step / supported. (No outcome guarantees.)

## 3. Archetype

**Guardian-Guide** (primary Caregiver/Guardian, secondary Sage-as-Guide). Rationale: protection must be *competent*, not fussy.

- **Logo:** calm, sheltering form, no weapon-like shield.
- **Visual:** steady, spacious, warm.
- **Voice:** a trusted neighbour who knows the subject.
- **UX:** proactive but never alarmist.
- **Photography:** capable farmers at work, not rescued ones.

## 6. Logo & Visual Identity

**Concepts explored:** (1) Shield-and-leaf, rejected as a cliché. (2) Compass needle, too generic and tech. (3) Bridge arc, too infrastructural. (4) Bunded field plot, weak at small size. (5) Signal rows, strong but cold. (6) **The Rakhan Mark (chosen).**

**The Rakhan Mark:** three concentric arches, like crop rows curving over a doorway, covering **one solid dot**. The arches read as shelter (the watch), signal waves (alerts, voice) and field rows (agriculture). The dot is the farmer and also the *next-step* point. Geometry: stroke equal to dot-diameter ÷ 2, round caps, arches spaced evenly, outer arch open at the bottom (the farmer is never enclosed or trapped). App icon: dot plus two arches on Regur Ink, Haldi dot. Reduces cleanly to dot + one arch at 16px. Wordmark: "ShetiRakshak" in a sturdy humanist sans with Devanagari **शेतीरक्षक** matched in weight; the pair always appears together.

**Shape and rhythm:** soft-structured; large radius, generous space, one focal element per screen. **Brand device — "The Watchline":** a thin horizon-like line with a Haldi dot marking *now/next*. It appears on weather timelines, price windows and scheme deadlines, which makes it recognisable without the logo. **Pattern:** subtle curved crop-row lines at low opacity, used sparingly.

**Data viz:** one message per chart; show the decision first ("फवारणीसाठी उद्या सकाळ योग्य"), data second. Weather as a day timeline marked with a decision dot; prices as a range with a date and source, not a hero number; schemes as a deadline Watchline; alerts as a left-edge colour bar plus an icon plus text (never colour alone).

## 8. Typography

- **Marathi/Devanagari + Latin UI:** **Mukta** (supports both scripts), fallback **Noto Sans Devanagari / Noto Sans** (Google fonts, free; covers Hindi and other Indic scripts for scale).
- **Scale (mobile):** Display 32/40 Bold · H1 26/34 · H2 22/30 · H3 19/28 (SemiBold) · Body **18/28 Regular** (larger than typical, for older users) · Caption 15/22 · Button 18/24 SemiBold · Numbers 24/28 SemiBold, tabular figures.
- **Rules:** Devanagari needs about 1.5× line-height; no all-caps in Latin; never below 15px; no light weights.

## 9. Multilingual System

- **Philosophy:** *Localise, don't translate.* Write natively in each language from a shared meaning brief; never machine-translate and ship.
- **Terminology:** a living, expert-approved glossary (Marathi farming terms first, the word farmers actually use over the textbook word); English technical terms stay only if farmers use them.
- **Numerals:** default per language (Devanagari numerals for Marathi), with a user toggle. **Units:** local (एकर, क्विंटल, °C) consistent everywhere. **Currency:** ₹ with Indian grouping (1,25,000). **Dates:** day-month, with weekday, plus the lunar/farming-season term only where it helps.
- **Voice:** accepts spoken Marathi regional variants; replies slowly, in short sentences, then repeats the next step.
- **Errors/buttons:** verbs, not nouns ("फोटो काढा", not "फोटो अपलोड").

## 10. Verbal Identity

**Voice (permanent):** a knowledgeable neighbour standing beside you. **Tone:** calm in alerts, encouraging in success, plain in errors, formal-respectful with scheme info. **Structure:** *What's happening → what it means for you → what to do next → how sure we are.* **Prefer:** शक्यता, तपासा, आधी, पुढचं पाऊल, तज्ज्ञ, अधिकृत स्रोत. **Avoid:** guaranteed, 100%, AI-powered, smart, revolutionary, "user", bureaucratic Sanskritised Marathi.

## 13. UX Principles (10)

1. **One screen, one decision.**
2. **Next step before more detail.**
3. **Marathi first, English second.**
4. **Voice when typing is hard.**
5. **Make uncertainty visible.**
6. **Name the source and the date.**
7. **A human is always one tap away.**
8. **Works on weak networks; show the last saved info.**
9. **Calm over clever; no noise, no clutter.**
10. **Never promise what we can't control.**

## 14. Home Screen Hierarchy

1. **Today's One Thing** (top, large): the single most important watch item with its next step (e.g., weather vs. planned spraying). Primary Haldi action: "काय करू?" / listen.
2. **Voice button**, persistent bottom center ("बोला").
3. **Crop watch strip:** current crop and stage, 1 line status (only after the farmer adds a crop).
4. **Next 3 days**, a minimal Watchline timeline.
5. **Deadlines** (schemes/market) shown only when relevant, max 1–2.
6. **Get help:** expert/KSK row, always visible but quiet.

Not shown by default: scheme directories, long price tables, feeds. Calm, focused, useful.

## 15. Accessibility

Touch targets ≥ 56dp (min 48dp); body ≥ 18px; contrast ≥ 4.5:1 (text), 3:1 (UI); icon + text + colour for status; voice for every core task; audio replies repeat the next step; offline-last-known data with timestamps; low-bandwidth mode (no video, compressed images, SMS fallback for alerts); max 3 taps to any core task; errors say what to do; one task per screen to reduce cognitive load.

## 16. Trust by Design: the Trust Strip

Every answer carries four compact signals:

- **Source:** "स्रोत: हवामान विभाग / कृषी अधिकारी" (only real, named sources).
- **Freshness:** "अद्ययावत: आज ७:१५".
- **Confidence:** three levels (below).
- **Human:** "तज्ज्ञांकडून तपासा" button when needed.

**Official information** gets a distinct "अधिकृत" badge and a link to the original; anything else is labelled "सामान्य माहिती". **Privacy:** plain-Marathi, short consent; say what is collected (photos, location), why, who sees it; easy delete. **Escalation rules:** low confidence, severe/spreading symptoms, treatment-dose questions, or user asks → stop guessing, offer expert/KSK, pass the photo and context.

## 17. Responsible AI Language

| Confidence | Marathi | English |
| --- | --- | --- |
| High | "ही शक्यता जास्त आहे" | "This is likely" |
| Medium | "ही एक शक्यता आहे" | "This is a possible issue" |
| Low | "खात्री नाही; तज्ज्ञांना विचारा" | "Not sure; ask an expert" |

**Never claim:** perfect diagnosis, guaranteed protection/profit/price/harvest/approval. **Always include:** "based on the information available", "verify before taking action", "official source". Treatment suggestions stay at *category* level and expert-reviewed; never invent doses.

## 18. Photography, Illustration, Icons, Motion

- **Photography:** natural morning/late-afternoon light, eye-level, farmer in the middle of capable work (checking a leaf, using a phone, talking with an expert); real cotton plants in stage-accurate detail; neutral warm grade with Ink shadows; never staged poses, pity or poverty.
- **Illustration:** mature, flat shapes with one line weight, realistic proportions, Ink + Haldi + muted crop tones; people shown in action and reading the situation; objects: boll, leaf, drop, wind, phone, handset, ledger.
- **Icons:** 2px stroke at 24px, 4px radius, rounded caps; outline default, filled = active; pictograms plus labels, never icon-only for critical actions.
- **Motion:** slow, gentle (200–300ms ease-out); the Haldi dot "arrives" to mark the next step; voice waveform forms from the arches; alerts slide in once, no looping; success = a quiet tick; respect reduced-motion.

## 19. Social Media System

Formats (tips, weather alerts, crop education, schemes, market info, expert advice, product updates, farmer stories) share one template: Kapus background, Ink headline in Marathi (max \~8 words), a Watchline motif, one Haldi dot on the key action, source and date footer, CTA verb-first ("सांगा", "तपासा"). Farmer stories need real consent; no invented testimonials.

## 20. Brand Story

Farmers have always kept watch: over clouds, leaves, markets, deadlines. But the watch has been lonely and scattered across apps, portals, videos and forwards. ShetiRakshak's belief is simple: farmers don't necessarily need more information. They need understandable information, at the right time, with a clear next step. So we keep watch with them, in Marathi, by voice, honestly. When we reach our limit, we bring a real person in. *(No founder story, statistics, partnerships or testimonials are invented here.)*

## 23. Do / Don't (core set)

**Do:** lead with the next step; speak Marathi natively; show source/date/confidence; use voice; show farmers as capable; label official info; offer a human; design for weak phones; use real crops; test with farmers; keep one decision per screen; use verbs on buttons; keep alerts rare and useful; give uncertainty a visible place; localise numerals/units; use Ink+Haldi consistently; get expert review; protect privacy plainly; respect elders' pace; ship an offline state. **Don't:** use leaf/tractor/shield clichés; say "guaranteed"; show helpless farmers; use saffron/fort tropes; bury the action; mimic government PDFs; use jargon; machine-translate; auto-diagnose without caveats; overuse notifications; use colour alone for status; gate help behind sign-up; fabricate testimonials or stats; show stale data unlabelled; use tiny text; use stock-photo poses; promote products as treatment without review; use AI-brain imagery; overstate partnerships; add features that crowd the home screen.

## 28. Naming Note

**Sheti + Rakshak** is short, rhythmic, easy to say in Marathi and Hindi, and the Sanskrit root is widely understood in India. Limits: English readers may hear "protector" as a military term, and "Rakshak" can feel authoritative. Mitigate with the Watch (a calm, accompanying protector) and with gentle voice. Keep the name; use "ShetiRakshak" with Devanagari "शेतीरक्षक" and consider future architecture like *ShetiRakshak Hawaman, Pik, Bazaar, Yojana*.
